# The Corner Cafe, Walmer — Local Concept Demo Brief

**Scope:** local, static concept demo only. No deployment, upload, publication, outreach, booking system, ordering, payment, account, live menu, or email sending.

## Workbench Read

Reading this as a location-led, owner-facing first website for a small family cafe in Walmer: warm, practical and quietly editorial rather than a generic brunch template.

## Audience and first-screen task

The owner should recognise a finished, shareable page for their cafe in five seconds. A nearby visitor should understand that it is a Walmer cafe for breakfast and lunch, with occasion-led afternoon tea and a straightforward way to call or find the cafe.

## Section hierarchy

1. Hero: business and place recognition; call and find-us actions.
2. Everyday table: breakfast, lunch and dietary choices framed as a visit rather than a static menu.
3. Occasion rhythm: afternoon tea and community occasions, without calendar promises.
4. The way of the cafe: small family cafe / local ingredients / dietary choices.
5. Visit panel: address, phone and an intentionally non-live route cue.
